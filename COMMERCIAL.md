# Workflow Intelligence — Commercial Path

The Community Edition is intended to maximize adoption without giving away every operational service around it.

## Community

Free, source-first, self-hostable Workflow Intelligence core with reproducible tests and public documentation.

## Cloud

A hosted service can monetize convenience and operations: persistent workspaces, collaboration, managed AI, backups, monitoring, reliability, and usage-based processing.

## Business

Paid organizational capabilities can include administration, advanced analytics, integrations, workflow monitoring, and priority support.

## Enterprise

Higher-value contracts can include SSO, advanced permissions, auditability, compliance tooling, private deployment, dedicated infrastructure, SLAs, enterprise governance, and custom integrations.

## Services

Implementation, workflow discovery, integration engineering, training, and proof-of-value programs can create additional revenue.

## Marketplace

A future integration ecosystem can support commercial connectors, templates, extensions, and partner offerings.

## Revenue rule

The business should measure actual cash collected, not projected customer value. A real payment is the threshold for claiming revenue validation.
